---
title: Prove work before calling it ready; never trade certainty for speed
kind: correction
applies_when: when deciding whether a piece of work is ready to hand to the owner
projects: all
evidence: 2026-10-16 owner ("We do not work for speed, only for certainty")
last_validated: 2026-10-16
status: active
---

Declare something ready only against stated, checked criteria: proofs run, negative tests that show each gate fails when it should, independent review, a fresh session succeeding from the docs alone. Then say so plainly.
Do not ship a v1 to hit a date, and promise no timelines.
Why: the owner would rather wait than get an unproven result, and leaves the judgement of "ready" to the agent.
