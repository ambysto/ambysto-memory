---
title: Look at zoomed art before shipping a character, logo or icon set
kind: workflow
applies_when: when a design agent or a session adds or replaces mascot, logo or icon art that a site shows, before merging or deploying it
projects: all
evidence: 2026-11-23 agentsocial PR 58 and the identity audit that followed
last_validated: 2026-11-23
status: active
---

Write the identity rules in one place before the art arrives (for a mascot: the letter style on the badge, the trim colours, what may change with the brand colour). Then open the new files zoomed on exactly those details and compare them with a reference image the owner gave, not with memory.
Colour and letter shape are different checks: matching the letter and the colour passed while the lettering was typeset instead of hand-drawn, and 29 files went live wrong.
Automated checks (contrast, aspect, icon counts, repeats) do not look at the character; do not report them as proof of identity. Say "looked at N files, not checked by a test".
Keep a list of every file with the rule it breaks and who owns the fix (art goes to the design agent, never drawn by the code session).
Confirm: a zoomed crop of each badge from every new file sits next to the reference and the owner has not corrected it.
