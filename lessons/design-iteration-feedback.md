title: Check the whole rendered design after each visual revision
kind: correction
applies_when: when iterating on a logo, icon or other visual design from owner feedback
projects: all
evidence: 2026-11-26 aipub-pm logo design and aipub-landing PR 83
last_validated: 2026-11-26
status: active
---

Keep the owner's accepted shapes, colours and relationships explicit; do not add features or change them without a request.
Translate each revision into a specific geometry or visual change: check which direction and dimension is meant, and ask only if the intended result cannot be inferred from context.
Render and inspect the full composition after edits at working and small intended sizes; check alignment, spacing, proportions and the interaction of elements, not just the edited path.
Compare with the actual supplied reference before claiming a match; distinguish genuine structural variations from colour-only variants and retain source and attribution details.
An SVG syntax check confirms the file parses, not that it looks right; report visual inspection and any untested uses separately.
This prevents repeated corrections such as shortening a letter too far, misaligning its dots or changing the wrong dimension while preserving an approved idea.
