Reply first with the conflict and a one-line question, with your guess of what was meant; act only after the answer.
Why: the owner runs many sessions at once and a message can land in the wrong one; acting on a slip wastes work and owner time.
Example: in the APS-31 session the owner wrote "focus on disk cleanup (Lean)"; the right reply was to note that this session's goal is APS-31, not to go looking for Lean tickets.
